### Introduction

Welcome to **Holdstation Pay** ("**Holdstation Pay**", "**we**", "**our**", or "**us**").

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Holdstation Pay collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your information when you use our fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat payment services (the "**Services**").

By accessing or using Holdstation Pay, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

> **Important:** If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue your use of the Services.

***

* Full name

We may also collect information regarding how you interact with the Services, including:

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information to:

* Provide Holdstation Pay Services
* Verify your identity
* Process fiat and cryptocurrency transactions

* Detect suspicious transactions
* Comply with AML/CFT obligations

* Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements

***

## 4. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Fraud Prevention

To comply with applicable Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF) regulations, Holdstation Pay may:

* Verify customer identity
* Verify ownership of bank accounts
* Request additional KYC documents
* Monitor transaction activities
* Screen users against sanctions lists
* Investigate suspicious activities
* Delay or suspend transactions where legally required
* Report suspicious activities to competent authorities where required by law

> **Note:** We reserve the right to request additional information at any time to comply with regulatory obligations.

***

## 5. Bank Account Ownership Verification

For security and compliance purposes, users must use bank accounts registered under the same legal name as their verified Holdstation Pay identity.

Transactions originating from third-party bank accounts may be:

* Rejected;
* Delayed for additional verification;
* Temporarily frozen;
* Refunded after completion of the verification process.

This measure helps prevent fraud, identity theft, and money laundering.

***

## 6. Blockchain Information

Blockchain transactions are inherently public.

When you use Holdstation Pay, blockchain-related information such as wallet addresses and transaction hashes may become publicly visible on the relevant blockchain network.

Holdstation Pay cannot modify, delete, or control information permanently recorded on public blockchains.
