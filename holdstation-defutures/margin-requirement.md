At Holdstation DeFutures, safety and responsible trading are paramount. Our margin system ensures a secure environment with the following features:

## Key Margin Details

* **Isolated Margin**: Default isolated positions limit risk to individual trades, allowing simultaneous long and short positions across any pair.
* **Asset-Specific Margins**: Each asset class has tailored margin requirements, outlined in the Leverage section.
* **Collateral and Leverage**: Customize position size by selecting collateral and leverage per trade.
* **Liquidation Price**: Automatically calculated, with adjustable stop-loss options to minimize risk.

We’re committed to a safe, secure trading experience, reflecting our dedication to responsible practices.
