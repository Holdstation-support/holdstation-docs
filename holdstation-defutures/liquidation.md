Holdstation DeFutures features an advanced execution bot dedicated to fulfilling limit orders and liquidations. This bot continuously monitors market conditions, executing trades instantly as prices align with set levels. Limit orders incur a **0.02% fee** to guarantee execution at your desired price, even during market volatility. These fees reward the executor, encouraging swift action.

### Key Details

* **Current Operations**: Three bots are active, ensuring efficient order fulfillment.
* **Future Expansion**: Plans include scaling executors via token hodling and enhanced decentralization.
