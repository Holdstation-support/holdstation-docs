## What is the Holdstation Seamless Key?

The **Seamless Key** is an **EVM-compatible delegated key** that powers frictionless trading while keeping funds safe.

* **Password Encrypted** – Generated locally and secured with your password.
* **Non-Custodial** – Keys never hold assets. Funds stay in your Master wallet (can be a hardware wallet).
* **Decentralized by Design** – Keys are created and stored locally, and can be regenerated anytime.
* **API-Ready** – Works as an API key for professional traders and automated bots.
* **Full User Control** – Create multiple Seamless Keys, manage permissions, or revoke them instantly from your Master wallet.

## What Am I Signing?

When generating a Seamless Key:

* You sign a combination of your **password + Master wallet address**.
* This ensures the Seamless Key is cryptographically linked to your Master wallet.
* Private key is generated locally, encrypted, and stored securely on your device.

## What Am I Authorizing?

When activating a Seamless Key, you authorize:

1. **USDC Permit** – Allowing Holdstation contracts to use your USDC as collateral.
2. **Seamless Key Enablement** – Granting the Seamless Key permission to execute trades.

## **Important:**

* Assets remain in your Master wallet at all times.
* Permissions are limited to trading functions only.
* Authorization can be revoked anytime.
* All setup and transactions are **gas-free**, sponsored by Holdstation relayer.
