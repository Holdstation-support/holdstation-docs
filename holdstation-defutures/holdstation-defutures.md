# 💹 Holdstation DeFutures

DeFutures, a flagship Holdstation project, pioneers the first **user-friendly** perpetual decentralized exchange, **powered by USD1**—World Liberty’s stablecoin. Built on **BNB Chain**, it features the **AI Matching Pool (AIMP)** to eliminate slippage, enabling precise matching of long and short positions with up to **100x leverage and ultra-low fees**. Across BNB, WLD, Berachain, Solana, and World Chain ecosystems. 

### Key Features

* **USD1-Powered Trading**: Leverage USD1 as collateral to bridge Trad-Fi and DeFi seamlessly, powering flexible trading positions.
* **Maximized Returns**: Stake USD1 to earn auto-compounded trading fees and gain points via World Liberty Finance for every trade or liquidity contribution, boosting passive income.

### Why Choose DeFutures?

* **Community-Focused**: Tailored for BNB Chain’s community with tools and incentives for growth and engagement.
* **Native Efficiency**: USD1 integration reduces costs and enhances usability, aligning with the ecosystem’s native structure.
* **Sustainable Ecosystem**: Rewards traders and liquidity providers, creating an inclusive, thriving DeFi environment.

DeFutures stands out as a dynamic platform, connecting innovation with community in BNB Chain’s DeFi landscape.
