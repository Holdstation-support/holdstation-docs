# 🍯 The HONEY Hub of Berachain

Holdstation is positioning itself as the **premier HONEY hub** within the Berachain ecosystem, creating a range of projects and utilities that not only leverage HONEY but also amplify its value, yield, and use cases. By centering its ecosystem around HONEY, Holdstation aims to contribute significantly to the adoption and liquidity of Berachain’s native stablecoin, fostering a thriving and interconnected DeFi environment.

## **HONEY-Centric Ecosystem**

Holdstation’s innovative products will seamlessly integrate HONEY, making it the backbone of its operations on Berachain. Here’s how:

### **beeFutures**

beeFutures, Holdstation’s rebranded on-chain futures platform, is tailored for the Berachain ecosystem and leverages HONEY as its core asset.

* **HONEY as Collateral**: Users can open leveraged positions by using HONEY as collateral, seamlessly integrating it into their trading strategies.
* **Liquidity Rewards**: Liquidity providers earn yield from trading fees, with additional rewards in **esHOLD** and **BGT** to maximize their returns.
* **DeFi Flywheel**: By utilizing HONEY as the default asset, beeFutures reinforces its utility across Berachain, driving demand and liquidity.

### **Holdstation Swap**

Holdstation Swap is Berachain’s strategic DEX, enabling seamless token swaps while prioritizing HONEY as a foundational trading asset.

* **HONEY Liquidity Pools**: Holdstation Swap will feature HONEY in its primary liquidity pairs, enhancing its liquidity and making it the go-to token for trading within Berachain.
* **Aggregator-Powered Efficiency**: As an aggregator, Holdstation Swap ensures the best rates for trades involving HONEY, encouraging its usage across the ecosystem.
* **Integration with Ecosystem Tokens**: By creating pairs with BERA, $HOLD, and project tokens launched via BeraStarter, Holdstation Swap ensures HONEY’s integration into Berachain’s broader DeFi landscape.

### **Holdstation Prediction Market**

The Holdstation Prediction Market offers a gamified yet practical utility for HONEY, where users can engage in outcome predictions for real-world and crypto events.

* **HONEY as the Default Asset**: Users place predictions using HONEY, creating a unique utility and locking up supply in active markets.
* **Yield Opportunities**: Point/Rewards collected from prediction markets are distributed back to HONEY liquidity providers and ecosystem participants, enhancing its value.
