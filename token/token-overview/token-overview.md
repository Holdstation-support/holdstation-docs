## **What is HOLD?**

HOLD is the native utility and governance token of the Holdstation ecosystem, designed to empower users while fueling the growth of DeFAI and blockchain applications. Serving as the backbone of Holdstation’s platform, HOLD integrates deeply with the ecosystem's key features, ensuring its value and utility extend across multiple use cases.

## **Key Utilities**

* **Governance Rights:**

HOLD token holders can participate in governance decisions, including voting on ecosystem upgrades, new product launches, and community initiatives. This ensures that the platform evolves in line with the community's vision.

* **Staking Rewards:**

Stake HOLD tokens to secure the ecosystem and earn attractive yields. Additionally, staking provides access to exclusive benefits, such as priority access to launches on Holdstation Launchpad. 

* **Fee Reduction:**

Holding HOLD to receive discounts fees across the Holdstation ecosystem, including smart wallet interactions, perpetual trading on DeFutures, and more.
