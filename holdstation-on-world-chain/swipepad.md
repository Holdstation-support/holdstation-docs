# ↔️ SwipePad

SwipePad, a standout side project within the Holdstation ecosystem, redefines token discovery with a Tinder-inspired twist. Launched recently, this innovative platform turns the mundane task of exploring crypto projects into a fun, fast, and social experience—swipe right to invest, swipe left to pass.

## Key Highlights

* **Swipe to Invest**: Unlock early access to trending tokens, maximizing profit potential with a seamless swipe mechanic.
* **Launch Your Own Token**: Create and deploy custom tokens on a fair bonding curve in minutes—no coding skills needed.
* **Human-First Approach**: Prioritizes verified users with unlimited purchase options, while capping non-verified allocations for fairness.
* **Track & Earn**: Monitor portfolio growth effortlessly and earn rewards as an active SwipePad community member.

## How to Degen ape with SwipePad

1. Download [World App](https://worldcoin.org/world-app) 
2. Connect a World ID-verified wallet.
3. Open SwipePad mini app inside the app.
4. Swipe left of right to buy/skip token. Choose "+" to create new token
