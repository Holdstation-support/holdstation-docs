### Wallet, Earn, and Explore — All in One

Holdstation combines three key layers for World Chain users.

#### 1. Wallet

Users can manage their World Chain assets in one simple place.

Holdstation supports token balances, sending, receiving, transaction history, swaps, and asset access through a mobile-first interface.

The wallet experience is built on top of World App’s Safe smart wallet infrastructure, with Account Abstraction support for gasless transactions on World Chain.

#### 2. Earn Hub

Users can join live earning campaigns directly from Holdstation.

Daily claims, vault campaigns, referral rewards, HOLD allocations, and partner quests help create a reason to return and stay active.

This is especially important for World App users who want simple, real, token-based opportunities instead of complicated DeFi products.

#### 3. Mini-App Gateway

Users can explore the World Mini-App ecosystem through Holdstation.

Holdstation connects users to partner apps, token discovery, quests, trading access, and ecosystem campaigns — helping them move deeper into World Chain over time.
