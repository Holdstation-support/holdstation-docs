# 🍭 Dalgona

Dalgona brings a delightful twist to the Holdstation ecosystem, blending classic candy-cutting challenges with real crypto rewards. Inspired by the viral Dalgona candy game, players carve perfect shapes from delicate honeycomb cookies without cracking them, testing steady hands, sharp focus, and quick thinking.

### Key Highlights

* **Skill-Based Gameplay**: Every move matters—master precision to succeed.
* **Rewarding Challenges**: Win $DAL and $WLD by conquering tasks, climb the leaderboard, and flaunt your candy-cutting prowess.
* **Real Rewards**: The better your performance, the bigger your $WLD and $DAL payouts.

### Performance Snapshot

* **Unique Opens (average 7 Days)**: 42,985, strong traction.
* **Ranking**: #16 on miniapps.world, a top-tier debut among 331 mini apps.
* **Ecosystem Synergy**: Complements Holdstation Wallet (#14 with 113,591 unique 7d opens), enhancing user engagement.
