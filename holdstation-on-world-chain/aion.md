# 💹 AION

## What is AION?

AION is an AI-powered mini app available within the World App ecosystem, designed to assist users in making informed decisions about Worldcoin (WLD) price movements. Developed by Holdstation, AION leverages advanced AI models to analyze real-time market data and provide predictions on whether the price of WLD will increase or decrease in the next five minutes.

## Key Highlights

* **AI-Driven Cashback**: Stake WLD on predictions; get 20% cashback in tokens if you’re wrong, easing the risk with every move.
* **Earn Big Rewards**: Correct predictions earn you profits from others’ stakes, plus chances to win prizes like an iPhone 16 Pro Max or $AION tokens.
* **User Control**: Follow AI forecasts or predict against them.
* **Instant Payouts**: Rewards hit your wallet right after each five-minute cycle.

## How to Access AION

1. Download [World App](https://worldcoin.org/world-app) 
2. Connect a World ID-verified wallet.
3. Navigate to **AION** inside the app.
4. Choose an asset (e.g. $WLD) and follow/counter AION’s prediction every 5 minutes.
5. Using $AION to earn cashback when you make a wrong prediction
