# 🥚 Eggs Vault: Gamified Rewards

## What is Eggs Vault?

**Eggs Vault** is a **gamified yield and reward engine** embedded within the Holdstation ecosystem. Designed to deepen user engagement and incentivize on-chain behavior, it transforms routine actions—like trading, staking, and using AI tools—into interactive quests and XP-based progression.

At its core, Eggs Vault serves as both a **reward distribution layer** and a **loyalty incentive system**, offering users tangible yield boosts and ecosystem perks through a dynamic, game-like interface. It integrates directly with World Chain’s real user base and extends across Holdstation’s trading suite (DeFutures, Swap, AION, etc.).

Whether you’re earning passive rewards or actively leveling up through daily tasks, **Eggs Vault is where on-chain performance meets off-chain fun**.

## How It Works

Eggs Vault operates on a **multi-phase progression system**, where users hatch and level up “eggs” by completing daily and long-term missions.

### Core Mechanics:

* **Daily Quests:**\
  Users are given simple missions like predicting with AION, making a swap, referring a friend, or providing liquidity. Completing these actions “charges” their egg.
* **Egg Hatching:**\
  Once the required XP is reached, the egg hatches into a reward—a yield boost, XP multiplier, referral boost, or token prize.
* **XP Progression System:**\
  As users accumulate XP, they advance tiers, unlocking larger and rarer rewards over time. The more they interact with Holdstation, the faster their progression.
* **Orb-Verified Integrity:**\
  Built natively for **World Chain**, Eggs Vault interacts only with **real, verified users** via **World ID + Orb**. This ensures rewards are distributed to legitimate actors, not bots or sybil attackers.

## How to Get Started

1. Access [Holdstation](https://holdstation.com/) via **World App**
2. Log in with your World ID or connected wallet.
3. Go to the **Eggs Vault** tab to view your daily quests and progress.
4. Complete tasks to charge your egg.
5. Hatch your egg, claim rewards, repeat daily.
