# 👁️ Why World Chain?

**World Chain** is the first _human-centric_ chain, purpose-built to prioritize real users over bots, and unlock the next billion in on-chain activity. Developed by the team behind Worldcoin and built on the OP Stack, World Chain is designed to be a permissionless public network that leverages biometric proof-of-personhood, real-world incentives, and a user-first execution layer.

## What Makes World Chain Unique?

World Chain is a Layer 2 network on Ethereum built by Tools for Humanity (TFH), leveraging three core innovations:

### 1. **Proof of Personhood (via World ID)**

World Chain uses biometric verification (via World ID and the Orb) to determine whether an address is operated by a real human. This enables:

* **Human-only airdrops**
* **Sybil-resistant voting and governance**
* **Prioritized blockspace for verified users**
* **Spam/bot-resistant social applications**

World ID is already being used by **over 5 million people across 160+ countries** (as of May 2025), and growing rapidly.

### 2. **Priority for Real Users**

In a typical L2, bots and MEV traders often dominate the blockspace. On World Chain:

* Verified users get **priority gas access** and **fee subsidies**
* Spam is deprioritized or blocked
* Builders can design apps knowing their users are real

This structure incentivizes the creation of real, useful apps instead of ponzi-style games optimized for bot farming.

### 3. **Local Blockspace + Shared Sequencer**

World Chain combines the decentralization and open access of Ethereum L2s with localized optimizations for verified users. Key points:

* Built using the **OP Stack** (the same tech behind Base and Optimism)
* Uses a **shared sequencer** model for greater interoperability and shared liquidity
* Can integrate future AI-native infrastructure for agent-based UX
