# ⛽ Paymaster

![](/.gitbook/assets/telegram-cloud-document-5-6251468772142485427.jpg)

At Holdstation, we leverage [zkSync's native Account Abstraction Paymaste](https://era.zksync.io/docs/reference/concepts/account-abstraction.html#paymasters)r to offer you an enhanced experience. Our Paymaster empowers users to:

1. Conveniently pay gas with stablecoins like USDC/USDT across all activities, from Swapping to Sending and Trading.
2. Experience gas-free trading positions, including Crypto ($5,000), Forex ($20,000), and Commodities ($20,000).
