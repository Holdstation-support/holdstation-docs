# 💳 Smart Wallet for Trading

At Holdstation, we go beyond execution, building a **Product Fit Community (PFC)** for traders with our Smart Wallet. Seamlessly integrated into **Holdstation DeFutures** and **Holdstation Pay**, it empowers users to start, scale, and succeed in trading with ease.

## Exclusive Features for Trading

Holdstation Wallet unlocks unique trading tools, transforming it into the ultimate **Trading Hub**:

* **DeFutures Integration:** Trade perpetual futures directly in-wallet with lightning-fast execution and deep liquidity.
* **Cross-Margin & Hedging:** Manage positions across assets, hedge risks, and maximize capital efficiency.
* **One-Tap Onramp with Holdstation Pay:** Instantly deposit VND (or local fiat) to buy USDT/USD1 and start trading in under 30 seconds.
* **PnL & Risk Tracking:** Monitor unrealized PnL, leverage ratios, and liquidation levels in real time.

## Seamless Integration and Interaction

* **Beginner-Friendly Access:** New users can onramp directly with Holdstation Pay and start trading without complex setup.
* **Unified Interface:** Swap, margin, and trade all in one wallet, eliminating the need for multiple platforms.
* **Smart Trade Routing:** Optimal execution with aggregated liquidity from connected venues.
